Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect your name and email address when you register, and the expense details you choose to enter (amounts, categories, dates and notes).

2. How we use it

Your information is used to run your account, show your expenses and summaries, and improve Spendly. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Storage and security

Passwords are stored in hashed form. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties except where required by law.

5. Your choices

You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you may ask for your account and data to be deleted.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Review our Terms and Conditions or contact the Spendly team.
